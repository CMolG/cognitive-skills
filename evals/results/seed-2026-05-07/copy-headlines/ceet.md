# Headlines

## Headline 1

Cut SRE on-call pages by triaging incidents from one trace.

For SRE teams paged on latency regressions in a service mesh, the
first ten minutes are spent finding the right trace. We surface it
before the page resolves.

## Headline 2

Logs and traces correlated by deploy, not by guesswork.

Most outage triage is reading logs in one tab and traces in another.
SREs at mid-sized SaaS teams stop bouncing between tools after the
first incident with us.

## Headline 3

Postmortems that write themselves from the actual evidence.

When the incident is over, the timeline of every dependent service,
every retry, every latency cliff is already in the doc. Write the
narrative, not the chronology.
